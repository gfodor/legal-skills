TECHNICAL FIELD

This invention relates to insulating sleeves for beverage containers.

BACKGROUND

Prior art beverage sleeves are of fixed diameter and therefore fit only one size
of container. Applicant is aware that U.S. Pat. 5,000,000 to Smith discloses a
foam sleeve, which fails completely to retain heat and is universally regarded
as a poor product. The essential problem with all prior sleeves is that they
cannot adjust.

SUMMARY

The invention is an adjustable insulating sleeve. It is the object of this
invention to provide a sleeve that fits multiple container sizes. It is a
further object to provide a sleeve that is cheap. The preferred embodiment uses
a nitinol tension band, which is essential to the invention.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Referring to FIG. 1, the sleeve 10 comprises a body 12 of closed-cell foam and a
tension band 14. The body 12 is generally cylindrical. The tension band 14 is
fitted within a channel 16 formed in the body 12.

The band 14 is made of Thermalok, a proprietary shape-memory alloy. The channel
16 receives the band 14. A clasp 18 secures the two ends of the band 14.

In FIG. 2, the cross-section shows the body 12 surrounding the channel 16. The
retaining ring 22 holds the assembly together.

OPERATION

In operation, the user slides the sleeve 10 over a container. The band 14
contracts, gripping the container. The clasp 18 may be released to remove the
sleeve 10.

CLAIMS

1. An adjustable insulating sleeve, comprising a cylindrical body of insulating
material, and a tension band disposed within said body, wherein the band is
substantially resilient.

2. The sleeve of claim 1, wherein the tension band comprises a shape-memory
alloy.

3. The sleeve of claim 1, wherein said clasp secures the ends of the tension
band.

4. The sleeve of claim 6, wherein the body is closed-cell foam.

5. A method of insulating a beverage container. The method comprises sliding the
sleeve of claim 1 over the container and allowing the band to contract.

ABSTRACT

An adjustable insulating sleeve for beverage containers is disclosed. The sleeve
has a cylindrical body of insulating material and a tension band disposed within
a channel in the body. The band is made of a shape-memory alloy and contracts to
grip containers of differing diameters. A clasp secures the ends of the band and
may be released to remove the sleeve from the container. The invention provides
an insulating sleeve that accommodates multiple container sizes, unlike prior
fixed-diameter sleeves which fit only a single container size and which are
therefore of limited utility to consumers who own beverage containers of more
than one diameter, a situation which is extremely common in ordinary households
and in commercial settings such as offices and restaurants where many different
vessels are in simultaneous use throughout the working day and evening.
