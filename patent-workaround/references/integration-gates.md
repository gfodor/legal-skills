# Outer-agent integration gates

Use these gates in order for every adversary candidate. The outer agent, not the
adversary, owns the final classification and any amendment.

## 1. Technical and commercial credibility

Reject or downgrade a candidate that depends on impossible behavior, hand-waves the
hard part, removes the indispensable outcome, transfers prohibitive cost or risk, or
assumes an actor will adopt an irrational workflow. Verify engineering assertions
against supplied evidence and primary technical sources when material.

Record:

- feasibility: `credible | marginal | not-credible | cannot-assess`;
- value retention: `materially-same | partial | different | cannot-assess`;
- assumptions that control either result;
- the likely competitor, buyer, user, and transaction.

Only `credible` or well-supported `marginal` candidates that retain materially the
same indispensable value proceed as material workarounds.

## 2. Claim coverage

Do an all-elements chart for every relevant independent claim. A dependent claim
cannot rescue coverage when its parent is not met, but a different independent claim
or claim class may. Address:

- exact limitation language and relationships, not a claim theme;
- governing construction posture and material definitions;
- direct actor and performance/assembly facts;
- divided, induced, contributory, importation, and extraterritorial theories only
  when the facts and current law make them relevant;
- pending-claim broadest reasonable interpretation separately from likely issued-
  claim construction;
- 35 U.S.C. 112(f) treatment and corresponding structure/acts when applicable;
- prosecution disclaimer or estoppel and related-family history;
- doctrine-of-equivalents screening under current law, limitation by limitation,
  including all-elements, vitiation, prior-art ensnarement, dedication, and
  prosecution-history constraints where relevant.

Never state a final infringement or noninfringement conclusion. Use:

- `apparently-captured`;
- `credible-literal-gap`;
- `equivalents-risk-only`;
- `construction-dependent`;
- `cannot-assess`.

If a candidate is apparently captured by any relevant independent claim, classify it
as `not-a-workaround` unless a material actor, territorial, or enforceability gap
remains.

## 3. Invention-umbrella classification

The umbrella is an evidence-based strategic patent-family or filing concept, not
legal claim scope and not a synonym for the current disclosure. Decide strategic
alignment before applying the support gate. Test each dimension separately:

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Motivating problem | Does the candidate address the same concrete problem, not merely the same market? |
| Design objectives | Does it preserve the same technical objectives, constraints, and tradeoffs? |
| Inventive mechanism | Does it use the disclosed mechanism, a supported generalization, or a sibling mechanism that the inventors actually possessed? |
| Deployed solution | Is it a substitute for, variant of, or boundary change to the actual implementation? |
| Commercial substitution | Would the same customer choose it for materially the same reason and transaction? |
| Disclosure nexus | Where does the application describe or suggest the mechanism, variation, or genus? |
| Business intent | Is the scope identified as indispensable, acceptable, or outside the user's objective? |

Use exact document locations and distinguish evidence from inference. Then assign one
classification:

- `not-a-workaround`: covered, implausible, or value-destroying;
- `outside-umbrella`: credible avoidance, but it materially changes the motivating
  problem, design objectives, indispensable value, commercial transaction, or
  user-defined intended patent-family scope;
- `umbrella-gap-supported`: credible avoidance within the intended contribution and
  supported enough to consider a capture strategy;
- `umbrella-gap-unsupported`: strategically aligned, but the authoritative disclosure
  does not establish possession or enablement of the needed scope, including an
  aligned newly identified mechanism that was not disclosed;
- `cannot-assess`: a controlling fact or construction is unavailable.

Lack of disclosure, different claim language, or a new mechanism does not by itself
place an alternative outside the strategic umbrella; those facts control the next
support gate. Conversely, sharing only a market or abstract result does not place an
alternative inside. Do not use a numeric score as a substitute for classification.
If dimensions conflict, explain which evidence controls and preserve the dissenting
facts.

## 4. Disclosure and procedural-posture gate

### Pending application

Map every proposed limitation and narrative sentence to the application as filed,
including drawings and originally filed claims. Analyze written description and
enablement across the full proposed scope. Prohibit new matter. Do not rely on an
inventor's later confirmation to retrofit support into the pending case.

### Pre-filing draft

Require authentic inventor-supplied technical facts for new mechanisms, examples,
alternatives, ranges, or generalizations. Label additions for inventor confirmation
and inventorship review. Do not fabricate an embodiment because it would improve
coverage.

### Issued patent or closed prosecution

Do not edit the patent as though it were pending. Identify possible continuation,
reissue, reexamination, disclaimer, or separate-filing questions for qualified
counsel, with applicable time, recapture, priority, intervening-rights, and new-
matter issues left expressly for current-law analysis.

Return `supported`, `partially-supported`, `unsupported`, or `cannot-assess` with
pinpoint citations and a separate enablement assessment.

## 5. Capture design

State the precise coverage objective before drafting. Prefer capturing the shared
technical mechanism or relationship that preserves the value over claiming the
desired result itself.

Consider, without automatically adding:

- removal of an accidental limitation;
- a supported genus with species fallbacks;
- structural and functional formulations supported by the disclosure;
- parallel independent claims for distinct actors, boundaries, or statutory classes;
- method steps placed on one actor when the invention and law support that choice;
- upstream, downstream, component, kit, consumable, configuration, and software-
  medium claims when commercially meaningful;
- dependent claims preserving current embodiments, ranges, algorithms, materials,
  topologies, and fallback combinations.

Avoid omnibus result claiming, a genus supported only by one narrow species,
unbounded functional language, claims whose performance necessarily spans unrelated
actors, and changes that erase the disclosed inventive mechanism.

## 6. Patentability and prosecution-risk gate

For every proposed independent-claim change and every materially broadened concept:

1. run a targeted prior-art search covering the new genus, omitted limitation,
   substitute, boundary, claim class, and combination;
2. verify full references, dates, and exact disclosure;
3. analyze current 35 U.S.C. 101, 102, 103, 112(a), 112(b), and 112(f), as applicable;
4. analyze effective filing date, priority support, inventorship, restriction,
   double patenting, unity, claim dependencies, statutory class, and formalities;
5. compare the new claim to the closest art limitation by limitation;
6. preserve fallback claims and explain what amendment territory or argument may be
   surrendered;
7. check related applications and prosecution history for inconsistent positions,
   estoppel, disclaimer, admissions, or foreseeable equivalents concerns.

For a pending application, route potentially material information uncovered by any
search or adversary to qualified counsel or another authorized person. Track the
source, routing date, current materiality/IDS/timing assessment status, and unresolved
facts in the search record and coverage ledger. Do not have the AI make the ultimate
materiality determination, sign an IDS, or submit anything. Treat unresolved counsel
triage for potentially material information as a promotion blocker.

Return one disposition:

- `draft`: adequately supported and no blocking rejection risk found in the recorded
  search, subject to residual limits;
- `narrow-or-layer`: potentially defensible only with narrower language or fallback
  claims;
- `narrative-support-only`: useful disclosure support but not presently safe or ready
  as a claim;
- `separate-filing-question`: may require a different application strategy;
- `deliberate-gap`: capture is unsupported, art-crowded, legally fragile, or outside
  the desired invention;
- `cannot-assess`.

"No blocking art found" is not a patentability guarantee. Identify search limits.

## 7. Narrative gate

Narrative amendments may clarify the problem, objectives, mechanism, technical
effects, embodiments, substitutions, boundary variations, and claim terminology.
They do not themselves prevent design-around.

Before adding narrative, ask:

- Is every technical fact supported for the current procedural posture?
- Does it enable the intended breadth rather than merely announce a result?
- Does it accidentally define an element, make an embodiment mandatory, disclaim an
  alternative, characterize prior art, or create an avoidable admission?
- Does disclosing an unclaimed alternative create dedication or other public-notice
  consequences under current law?
- Is there a corresponding present or planned claim strategy?
- Does the edit change inventorship, priority, best-mode, or duty-of-disclosure facts?

Label each proposed sentence `claim-support`, `construction-clarity`,
`embodiment-support`, `background-context`, or `risk-only`. Delete prose with no
clear purpose.

## 8. Promotion gate

Before promoting a candidate, require:

- full clean application and redline;
- claim-status and dependency table;
- all-elements charts for the addressed workarounds;
- exact as-filed or inventor-confirmed support map;
- targeted prior-art search record and risk analysis;
- coverage ledger with every finding dispositioned;
- inventor/applicant decisions on commercial tradeoffs;
- full render and mechanical checks;
- exact packet hash matching the reviewed candidate.
- no unresolved potentially material information awaiting counsel/IDS/timing triage.

A promise to draft or verify later is not a promotable candidate.
