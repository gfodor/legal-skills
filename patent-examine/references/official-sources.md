# Current-law and prior-art source hierarchy

Refresh these sources on every run. Record the access date, displayed revision date,
and any later examination memorandum not yet incorporated into the MPEP. Do not rely
on the dates in this file as proof that a page remains current.

## Controlling and authoritative sources

1. **Statutes**: U.S. Code, Title 35, especially sections 100-103, 111-123, 132,
   253, and 282. Use the current text from the U.S. House Office of the Law Revision
   Counsel: <https://uscode.house.gov/browse/prelim@title35&edition=prelim>.
2. **Rules**: current 37 CFR Part 1 in eCFR, especially 1.56, 1.75, 1.78, 1.104,
   1.105, 1.111, 1.121, 1.132, and 1.113:
   <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1>.
3. **Binding precedent**: current U.S. Supreme Court and Federal Circuit opinions.
   Use official court PDFs/opinions where available; verify later treatment before
   relying on a case.
4. **USPTO procedure and guidance**: current MPEP plus later binding examination
   memoranda. The MPEP describes Office procedure but is not itself substantive law.

## Prior-art sources

Use broad discovery tools, then preserve and verify the actual primary reference:

- USPTO Patent Public Search: <https://ppubs.uspto.gov/pubwebapp/>
- USPTO Patent Center for public file wrappers:
  <https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/applications/search>
- WIPO PATENTSCOPE: <https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/search.jsf>
- EPO Espacenet: <https://worldwide.espacenet.com/>
- NPL from publishers, standards bodies, repositories, product manuals, archives,
  and other evidence with verifiable public-availability dates.

Google Patents and ordinary web search may help discover references, but verify the
bibliographic record, family, dates, and relied-on disclosure against the patent
publication or an official database. Preserve PDFs or stable full-text copies.

## Source record fields

For every legal or prior-art source used in a rejection, record:

```text
source_id:
title:
issuing_authority_or_publisher:
url_or_publication_number:
version_revision_date:
publication_or_public_availability_date:
priority_and_effective_prior_art_date:
accessed_at:
pin_cites:
purpose:
verification_notes:
```

If a source cannot be verified, exclude it from a prima facie rejection and state
the resulting search limitation.
