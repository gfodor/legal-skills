## Claim construction under review

State the material BRI and its basis. Identify any construction uncertainty. Do not
import a feature merely because the specification describes it, and do not erase an
express structural, relational, conditional, or sequential claim requirement.

## Reference substance synopsis

For each reference, identify:

- the operative system/process actually taught and its technical objective;
- the relied-on embodiment, examples, figures, and reference numerals;
- the flow of material, data, control, or method steps relevant to the claim;
- alternatives that are separate from the relied-on embodiment;
- counterteachings, incompatibilities, or context material to the asserted mapping;
- whether the full text, figures, dates, and any translation were verified.
